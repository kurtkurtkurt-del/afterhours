privacy.

In short

afterhours works without an account: you can look at the nights, the map and the djs as a guest. With an account (or a guest session in the app) we keep only what the product needs to work: your email, your handle, the nights you kept, your check-ins, your friendships, and what you wrote in a room. Nothing you let go is ever shown to anyone. Who liked what is open to nobody, not even to us one by one.

What we keep, and why

Email and password — to sign you in. Stored by Supabase (our database and authentication provider, servers in Frankfurt, EU). Guests in the app get an anonymous account with no email.
Profile — handle, display name, one line, home city. Your handle is how friends find you; you can turn off being findable by strangers in settings.
Swipes — kept and let-go nights, so the deck does not repeat itself. Kept nights are visible to confirmed friends only if you allow it. Let-go nights are visible to no one.
Check-ins — that you were at a night, the time, and (only if you allowed location) where you stood, so the card can be issued. Visible to you, to confirmed friends if you allow it, and as an initial to the others in the same room.
Room posts — up to 200 characters, readable only by the people who checked in to the same night, frozen 48 hours after the night.
Friendships — requests and confirmations.
Location — in the app, only while it is open, only when you allow it, to show nights near you and to check in. It is not stored except as part of a check-in.
Background music — off by default; your choice of genre is stored on your device only.

What we do not do

No advertising, no advertising id, no analytics sdk, no tracking across apps or sites, no selling or sharing of personal data. The app talks to exactly three services: Supabase (database, sign-in, files), Ticketmaster (public event listings, no personal data sent), and Esri (map tiles for the area you look at; no personal data is sent, only the tile coordinates).

Your rights

You can see everything we hold about you in the app (settings → download my data) or on this site (settings → export). You can delete your account in the app (settings → delete account) or on this site (settings). Deletion removes your profile, swipes, check-ins and friendships at once; texts you posted stay, with your name replaced by "someone". Under the GDPR you also have the right to correction, restriction, portability and objection; write to the address in the impressum.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub B.V., Amsterdam). Server logs (ip address, time, requested file, browser) are processed by GitHub for security and stability. The app is distributed through Google Play and, later, the App Store; their own privacy terms apply to the download.
