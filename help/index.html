how this
works.

one card. one night.

Swipe right to keep it, left and it never comes back. No search, no feed. The deck is in the app; on the web the same nights hang on the wall, soonest first.

go, and it becomes a card.

Check in at the door. The night turns into an afterhours card, yours to keep — who was there on the front, what the night sounded like on the back.

the room closes in 48 hours.

Who was there talks there: two lines, a voice note (soon). Then it freezes, forever. Before the night there is beforehours: what people say ahead of it, open to everyone, written with an account.

see who is going.

Your friends' kept nights, who is coming — i'm in, maybe, not tonight — and a match when you both keep the same one. What you let go is never shown to anyone.

what is near you, right now.

The map shows the nights around you tonight. Szene nights open their address at check-in.

on the web

The wall (explore), every night's page with beforehours, host an event, the collection, your account and its settings, and this page. The deck, the map, your people, messages and the collection live in the app — get it on google play. A night's web page opens the same night in the app with "open in the app"; a night shared from the app opens its web page.

something wrong?

A night with the wrong date or one that has already happened — write it in beforehours under that night, or send feedback. It gets read.
