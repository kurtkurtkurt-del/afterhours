the deck

One card at a time, and no search anywhere on this site — that is deliberate. Throw a card right to keep it, left and it is gone for good. Drag it, or use the arrow keys. The three sources on the left change what the deck is made of: everything in the city, what your friends kept, or the same cards shuffled.

beforehours

What people said before a night, split into this week and earlier ones. Reading needs nothing. Writing needs an account.

friends & more

Pick a handle so people can find you, add friends by theirs, and see everything you have kept in one place. What a confirmed friend keeps turns up in your deck — what they passed on never does, and neither does anyone else's.

card collection

Where the nights you actually went to end up — the sounds, the conversations, what was said at four in the morning. Empty for now; it is the next thing being built.

your account

Sign in with an email and a password. Swiping without an account works too — it is kept in this browser and moves to your account the moment you sign in. Which cards you kept is yours; only confirmed friends see it, and nobody sees what you passed on.

something wrong?

An event with the wrong date or a night that has already happened — write it in beforehours under that event. It gets read.
