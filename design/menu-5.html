what this would mean

the wall goes. the web no longer has its own way of showing nights; the deck is the one way, in the app and in the browser. explore/ becomes a redirect into the web app's flow tab. the README's “two surfaces” becomes one surface with a door in front.

the closed pages go. friends/, maps/, djs/ and their “only available in the app” with the phone preview are no longer needed: the thing itself is one click away.

the pwa must carry it. today the pwa is the expo app in a browser. for this design it has to work signed out (the deck in read-only), take a night link, install cleanly on ios and android, and be as fast as the plain html it replaces. this is the real cost — weeks, not days.

the site keeps four jobs. the door (landing + store links), hosting, the account forms, the legal pages. these are the pages that search engines and e-mails land on, and they stay plain html with no build step.

the words line up by removal. there is no second vocabulary left to align: the app's five words are the product, the site's four words are the frame around it.
