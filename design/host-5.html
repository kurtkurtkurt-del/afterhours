host an event — five flows

Four steps each, left to right. The first question is the same in all five: who is this night for — ticketed (a link to tickets, open to everyone), friends (only people in your circle see it; the address shows after a yes), open door (everyone, no tickets). Then the facts, then the poster, then it is live.
