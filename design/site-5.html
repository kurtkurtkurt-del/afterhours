what they share

Five tabs, app tokens, lowercase headings, no decoration, every number computed from live data (never typed). Night pages, person cards and group invites look the same in all five; only home, nights and host change.

what each one costs

1 needs posters for every night (Ticketmaster images cover ~70%; the rest get the generated poster). 2 and 3 are the cheapest to build and the easiest to keep fast. 4 needs the local hour and open/close times per night (we have doors; closing is estimated). 5 is the most work (the web deck already exists in the PWA, but the no-login variant is new) and the most fun.
